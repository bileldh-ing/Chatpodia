# Chatpodia Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

**Your data stays on your device.** Chatpodia has no backend of its own.

- **Account & chats.** Your name, email, password hash (PBKDF2-SHA256, salted) and conversations are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. Session tokens and API keys are stored in the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain.
- **AI requests.** When you send a message, its text (and any image you attach) is sent to the AI provider you selected in Settings — Pollinations (default), Google Gemini, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint — solely to generate a reply. Their privacy policies apply to that request.
- **Voice.** Speech recognition uses your device's built-in recognizer.
- **Google Sign-In (optional).** Only your name, email and profile photo are read to create the local account.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.** Diagnostics logs stay in memory on the device and are only shared if you copy them yourself.
- **Deletion.** Settings → Delete account removes your account and every conversation from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
