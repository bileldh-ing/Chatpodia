Chatpodia is a beautifully crafted AI assistant with a liquid-glass interface.

• Chat with Podia — streaming answers, code blocks with one-tap copy, quick replies
• Voice mode — talk naturally, see your words appear live, hear answers read aloud
• Create images — describe anything and get a high-quality image in seconds
• Build web pages & mini apps — preview them live inside the app
• Motion clips — Podia writes a storyboard and animates it into a short clip
• Attach photos and ask questions about them (Gemini / OpenAI)
• Private by design — your account and chats are stored on your device, protected by an optional fingerprint / face lock
• Works free out of the box, or bring your own Gemini / OpenAI-compatible key
